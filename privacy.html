Privacy Policy

How Milestone Development collects, handles, and safeguards your personal data in accordance with the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth) and Australian Privacy Principles.

1. Information We Collect

When you submit an enquiry or contact Milestone Development via our website, telephone (1800 008 883), or email (admin@milestonedevelopment.com.au), we collect personal information necessary to respond to your project requirements. This may include your full name, contact phone number, email address, project location, and development scope preferences.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your submitted details exclusively for professional construction and development communication, including:

  • Responding to project enquiries and quotation requests
  • Providing feasibility assessments and project management communications
  • Fulfilling legal, contractual, and regulatory compliance obligations

We do not sell, rent, or lease your personal information to third parties.

3. Data Protection & Security

We implement industry-standard security measures, including HTTPS encryption, HSTS protocols, and restricted system access, to protect your personal data from unauthorized access, alteration, or disclosure.

4. Access & Correction Rights

Under Australian privacy law, you have the right to request access to the personal information we hold about you or request corrections. To submit a request, please contact our team via email at admin@milestonedevelopment.com.au or by calling 1800 008 883.
