Builder-led delivery

Construction expertise from early planning to handover.

Milestone Development is an Australian construction and property development company supporting clients across residential, commercial and industrial projects. From our North Sydney base, the team brings practical construction thinking to design coordination, site planning, buildability, program control and delivery.

Every project begins with a clear understanding of the site, intended use, budget priorities and required finish. Builder-led oversight helps connect those early decisions with approvals, procurement, construction sequencing, quality checks and formal handover.
